# skills.sh Programming-Principles Catalog Findings

This review checked the public `skills.sh` catalog on 2026-09-12. The catalog search endpoint was the public legacy endpoint documented by the CLI source: [`/api/search`](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills/blob/main/src/find.ts). The results are community catalog entries and do not imply that skills.sh endorses their content.

## Findings

| Principle or area | Result | Representative catalog evidence |

## Bottom Line

The catalog contains at least one exact or explicitly named skill for every requested term. For a broad, code-focused set covering several principles together, [JordanCoin/codingskills](https://github.com/JordanCoin/codingskills) is the clearest match because its repository describes separate KISS, DRY, SOLID, and YAGNI skills and how they work together. For broader design work, [software-design-philosophy](https://skills.sh/wondelai/skills/software-design-philosophy) and [codebase-design](https://skills.sh/mattpocock/skills/codebase-design) are stronger matches than the task-orchestration divide-and-conquer entries.
